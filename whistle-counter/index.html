Privacy Policy — Cooker Whistle Counter (A3 Enterprises)

Last updated: 23 October 2026

1. Microphone: The app uses your microphone only while you start counting or calibrating. Audio is analysed in real time on your device to detect cooker whistles. Audio is NEVER recorded to storage, NEVER uploaded and NEVER shared. During calibration a few seconds of sound are held in memory, reduced to a single pitch number, and discarded.

2. Data stored on your device: your target count, presets and calibration pitch. Nothing leaves your phone.

3. Ads: The free version shows Google AdMob banner ads. Google may collect device identifiers for ad delivery under its own policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Buying Pro removes ads.

4. Purchases are handled by Google Play; we do not see your payment details.

5. Children: the app is not directed at children under 13.
